Chrome only lets you change extension shortcuts on its own settings page. Find QuickTag there, click the pencil icon next to the shortcut box, then press the keys you want.

Use Ctrl or Alt with a letter or number, for example Alt+Shift+Q. Avoid shortcuts your browser already uses, such as Ctrl+T or Ctrl+Shift+N.
